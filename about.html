Wholesale Squishy Supplier, Based in the USA

South Gate, California

Wholesale Squishy is a squishy supplier for the people who resell or restock squishy toys at scale: retailers, gift and novelty shop owners, bulk vending and scoop-machine operators, and procurement buyers who want to buy squishies in bulk without going through an overseas importer. We currently stock two case-pack lines — dumpling-shaped squishies and fruit & novelty squishies — and ship every order from our own US warehouse.

Why Wholesale Squishy

Buying squishies one at a time is expensive and slow to restock. We sell wholesale squishies by the case, with per-piece pricing that drops as order size goes up, so resellers and classrooms can stock up without paying retail markup on every unit.

What We Offer

  • Case-pack pricing on our two current lines: dumpling & bun squishies and fruit & novelty squishies
  • Custom quotes for larger wholesale orders
  • Fast US shipping, 1 business day processing

Who We Serve

Toy stores, gift shops, school suppliers, classroom teachers, party planners, vending operators, and online resellers on Amazon, eBay, and TikTok Shop — anyone sourcing wholesale squishies USA-based rather than a slow overseas shipment.
