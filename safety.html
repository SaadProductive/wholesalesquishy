Children’s Product Certificate (CPC) on file

Our squishy toys are supplied under a Children’s Product Certificate, based on testing by a CPSC-accepted laboratory (Zhejiang RTS Test Co., Ltd., CPSC lab ID 1422). Age grade tested: 3 years and up.

Download the certificate (PDF)
PDF, 2 pages. Importer contact details are redacted for privacy.

What the certificate covers

Tested to the following US requirements:

  • Lead in children’s products (CPSIA Section 101)
  • Phthalates in children’s toys (16 CFR 1307) and ASTM F963-23 Section 4.3.8
  • ASTM F963-23 toy safety standard: substrate materials, mechanical hazards, flammability, labeling
  • Small parts labeling for ages 3 to 6 (FHSA, 16 CFR 1500.19)
  • Tracking labels (CPSA Section 14(a)(5))
