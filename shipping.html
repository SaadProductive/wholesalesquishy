Shipping Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Wholesale Squishy currently ships to addresses within the United States only. This is a draft policy for legal review before publishing. It is not a substitute for advice from a licensed attorney.

1. Processing Time

Orders ship within 1 business day of being placed. Orders placed on weekends or holidays are processed the next business day.

2. Carriers & Delivery Estimates

MethodCarrierEstimated Transit
StandardUSPS Ground Advantage3–5 business days
ExpeditedUPS 2-Day2 business days

Delivery estimates begin once an order ships, not from the order date, and are estimates rather than guarantees. Carrier delays are outside our control.

3. Shipping Costs

Shipping cost is calculated at checkout based on order weight and destination. Orders over $150 qualify for free standard shipping.

4. Order Tracking

You'll receive a tracking number by email once your order ships.

5. Lost or Delayed Packages

If tracking shows no movement for 5+ business days or your package is marked delivered but not received, contact us and we'll work with the carrier to locate it or send a replacement.

6. Bulk & Wholesale Orders

Large wholesale orders may ship via freight or in multiple shipments; timelines are confirmed with you at the time of quote.
