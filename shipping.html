Shipping Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Wholesale Squishy currently ships to addresses within the United States only.

1. Processing Time

Orders are processed immediately and delivered within 5 working days. Orders placed on weekends or holidays are processed the next business day.

2. Carriers & Delivery Estimates

Orders ship by standard ground service (USPS Ground Advantage or UPS Ground, our choice by weight and destination). Most orders are delivered within 5 working days of ordering; distant states or carrier delays can take up to 8 business days. Delivery times depend on distance from our California warehouse. Delivery estimates are estimates rather than guarantees. Carrier delays are outside our control. Expedited service is not offered at checkout; email us if you need a faster option for a specific order.

3. Shipping Costs & Minimum Order

There is no minimum order value; the smallest order is one case, and you can mix any styles. Shipping is calculated automatically in your cart and at checkout for addresses in the continental United States. It is based on the number of cases ("boxes") in your order: $12 plus $2.50 per case, to a maximum of $400.

4. Order Tracking

You'll receive a tracking number by email once your order ships.

5. Lost or Delayed Packages

If tracking shows no movement for 5+ business days or your package is marked delivered but not received, contact us and we'll work with the carrier to locate it or send a replacement.

6. Bulk & Wholesale Orders

Large wholesale orders may ship via freight or in multiple shipments; timelines are confirmed with you at the time of quote.
