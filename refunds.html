Refund Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Because all sales are final (see our Return Policy), refunds are issued only in the cases below.

1. When We Refund

  • We cancel your order (for example, an item is out of stock or a pricing error): full refund
  • Your order arrives damaged, incorrect or short and you report it within 7 days of delivery with photos: replacement or refund of the affected items
  • A package is lost in transit and cannot be replaced: refund of the order

2. No Refunds for Change of Mind

We do not refund orders for change of mind, slow sell-through, overstock or ordering the wrong style or quantity.

3. Refund Method

Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method. We do not issue cash refunds.

4. Processing Time

Once approved, refunds are issued within 5 business days. Depending on your bank or card issuer, it may take an additional 3 to 10 business days for the refund to appear on your statement.

5. Shipping Costs

Original shipping charges are refunded only when the refund is due to our error, a damaged or incorrect order, or a cancelled order.
