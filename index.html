FAQ

Questions bulk buyers actually ask

What's the minimum order?
There is no minimum order value. The smallest order is one case (6 or 12 pieces depending on the style), and you can mix any styles. Volume pricing starts at 50 pieces.
Are these safety-tested for retail and vending use?
Yes. Our toys are supplied under a Children's Product Certificate, tested by a CPSC-accepted lab to CPSIA and ASTM F963-23. Download the certificate (PDF) or see the safety page. Email support@wholesalesquishy.com if you need a certificate matched to specific styles.
How fast does it ship?
Orders are processed immediately and most arrive within 7 working days from our US warehouse.
Can I get a custom quote for a large order?
Yes, orders over 500 pieces per style get carton-level pricing.
Can you invoice a business or procurement team?
Yes. Send us your SKUs and quantities and we issue an invoice payable by Stripe, Wise or US bank transfer. Payment is in full before shipping, and bulk orders ship about 7-8 working days after payment. Use the quote request page or email support@wholesalesquishy.com.
