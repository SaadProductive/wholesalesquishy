Return Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

All sales are final. We do not accept returns or exchanges. Please check your styles, case sizes and quantities before you pay. If you are unsure, email us first and we will help you choose.

1. No Returns for Change of Mind

We do not accept returns, exchanges or cancellations after an order has shipped, including for slow sell-through, overstock, change of mind or ordering the wrong style or quantity. Orders are processed immediately, so cancellation before shipment is only possible if you email us right away and the order has not been packed.

2. Damaged, Incorrect or Short Orders

If your order arrives damaged, you received the wrong item, or the count is short, email support@wholesalesquishy.com within 7 days of delivery with your order number and clear photos of the cases and the shipping box. After review, we will send a replacement or refund the affected items at our discretion, at no cost to you.

3. Lost Packages

If tracking shows no movement or the package is marked delivered but not received, contact us and we will work with the carrier. See our Shipping Policy.

4. Refunds

Refunds, where they apply, are described in our Refund Policy.
