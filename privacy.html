Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Wholesale Squishy ("Wholesale Squishy," "we," "us," or "our") operates wholesalesquishy.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. This is a draft policy for legal review before publishing. It is not a substitute for advice from a licensed attorney familiar with your specific business.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you give us directly, such as your name, email address, shipping and billing address, phone number, and order details when you place an order, request wholesale pricing, or sign up for restock alerts. We do not ask for or store full payment card numbers. Card payments are processed by Stripe, which collects and secures that data under its own privacy and security terms.

We also collect limited technical information automatically, including IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited, through standard server logs and analytics tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfill orders, including shipping, invoicing, and customer support
  • To respond to wholesale pricing requests and custom quote inquiries
  • To send order confirmations, shipping updates, and, only if you opt in, restock and promotional emails
  • To improve the Site, catalog, and checkout experience
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers who need it to operate the Site and fulfill orders, for example Stripe (payment processing), shipping carriers (delivery), and email or analytics providers, and only to the extent required for those services. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

4. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep your cart contents, remember preferences, and understand how the Site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect cart and checkout functionality.

5. Data Retention

We retain order and account information for as long as needed to fulfill orders, comply with tax and accounting obligations, and resolve disputes, and then delete or anonymize it in line with our internal retention schedule.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your state of residence, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and to opt out of marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

Wholesale Squishy sells to businesses and adult consumers. The Site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
