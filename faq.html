Frequently Asked Questions

 

Is there a minimum order?

There is no minimum order value. The smallest order is one case (6 or 12 pieces depending on the style), and you can mix any styles. No account or application is needed to order.

Do you ship outside the US?

Not yet. We currently ship to addresses within the United States only.

How long does shipping take?

Orders are processed immediately. Most orders are delivered within 5 working days; distant states or carrier delays can take up to 8 business days. Shipping is about $12 plus $2.50 per case and free from $5,000; orders above $15,000 ship by freight, quoted separately. See our Shipping Policy.

Can I return a case if it doesn't sell well?

Unopened, unused cases can be returned within 30 days of delivery. See our Return Policy for condition requirements.

Do you offer custom quotes for large orders?

Yes. Email support@wholesalesquishy.com with the quantity and products you need, or use the Download Catalog (PDF)Request an Invoice / Bulk Quotesupport@wholesalesquishy.com page.

Is checkout live right now?

Checkout runs on real Stripe payment processing. If anything looks off during checkout, contact us directly and we will get your order sorted.
