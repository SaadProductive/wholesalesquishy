Frequently Asked Questions

 

Is there a minimum order for wholesale pricing?

No separate account needed — case-pack (wholesale) pricing applies starting from a single case.

Do you ship outside the US?

Not yet. We currently ship to addresses within the United States only.

How long does shipping take?

Orders process within 1 business day. Standard USPS Ground Advantage typically arrives in 3–5 business days; UPS 2-Day arrives in 2 business days. See our Shipping Policy.

Can I return a case if it doesn't sell well?

Unopened, unused cases can be returned within 30 days of delivery. See our Return Policy for condition requirements.

Do you offer custom quotes for large orders?

Yes — email support@wholesalesquishy.com with the quantity and products you need, or use the Wholesale & RFQ page.

Is checkout live right now?

This storefront is currently a demo build — checkout does not process real payments yet. Reach out directly for real orders in the meantime.
